🚀 **Excited to share my latest full-stack project: SparePlug!** 🚗💨

Finding authentic auto spare parts can be a hassle—endless calls, visits to multiple shops, and uncertainty about quality. I built **SparePlug** to solve this.

**SparePlug** is a multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace designed specifically for the auto parts industry in Cameroon. It bridges the gap between vehicle owners and verified local vendors, making the search for parts fast, transparent, and direct.

💡 **Key Features:**
✅ **Smart Search Engine:** Filter parts instantly by Vehicle Make, Model, Year, and Price.
✅ **Vendor Dashboard:** A complete management suite for shop owners to track inventory, views, and inquiries.
✅ **Direct WhatsApp Integration:** Buyers can chat directly with sellers with one click—bridging the gap between digital discovery and personal negotiation.
✅ **Tiered Subscriptions:** Built-in logic for Free, Basic, and Premium vendor plans.

🛠️ **Tech Stack:**
*   **Backend:** PHP (Custom MVC Architecture)
*   **Database:** MySQL (Optimized with complex joins & indexing)
*   **Frontend:** Tailwind CSS & Vanilla JavaScript
*   **Integration:** WhatsApp Business Logic

This project challenged me to think deeply about **database normalization**, **secure authentication**, and creating a **mobile-first UX** for a real-world market need.
