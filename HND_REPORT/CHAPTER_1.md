# CHAPTER ONE: INTRODUCTION AND PRESENTATION OF ENTERPRISE

## 1.1 Background to the Study

This chapter set the stage for the whole project. I explained where the idea came from and why I chose this topic. I also talked about the problem I wanted to solve and what I hoped to achieve at the end.

### 1.1.1 Historical Background

The spare parts market in Cameroon has changed a lot over the years. When cars first came to Yaoundé, people had to go to special markets to find parts for their vehicles. The biggest markets were Mvo-Mbi and Madagascar. These markets have been there for many years and still operate the same way today.

According to DataReportal (2025), Cameroon now has about 15 million people who use the internet. This means more people are getting online every day. But even with this growth, most spare parts shops in Yaoundé still write everything down in paper books. They do not use any computer system.

The history of car parts trading in Cameroon started from the 1980s when lots of used cars came from Europe. At first, shop owners just remembered what they had in stock. But as more car models came to the country, it became very hard to remember everything. This led to the problems I wanted to solve with my project.

### 1.1.2 Conceptual Background

A web-based car parts system is a website where people can search for car parts, see if they are available, and compare prices from different sellers. This type of system is very popular in other countries. For example, in Europe there is AUTODOC and in America there is RockAuto. These websites help car owners find the exact parts they need very quickly.

In this project, I called my system **SparePlug**. SparePlug is a platform that connects car owners with spare parts sellers in Yaoundé. The system allows vendors to list their products with prices and pictures. Buyers can then search for what they need and compare prices before they go to buy.

The main idea behind SparePlug is to solve the problem of people walking around for hours just to find one part. With this system, a person can sit at home and search on their phone. They can see exactly what is available and how much it costs. This saves them time and money.

### 1.1.3 Theoretical Background

To build this system the right way, I needed to use some theories that already exist. Two important theories guided my work.

The first one is called the **Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)**. This theory was created by Fred Davis in 1989. He said that people will use a new technology if they think it is useful and if it is easy to use. If people in Yaoundé think SparePlug is too hard to use, they will not use it. So I had to make the system very simple.

The second theory is the **Information Systems Success Model** by DeLone and McLean. This model helped me understand what makes a system successful. It says that the system must be fast, the information must be correct, and users must be happy with it. I used these ideas to make sure SparePlug works well.

Research shows that these models are very important for technology adoption in Africa. A study by Hossain, Hussain, and Akther (2023) found that trust and ease of use are key factors for e-commerce in developing countries. This matches what I wanted to do with SparePlug.

### 1.1.4 Contextual Background

This project came from my observations of the spare parts market in Yaoundé during 2024. While doing my internship in the IT sector, I noticed that many car parts shops still use very old methods to manage their business. Most of them write everything in paper books and remember prices from memory.

One shop that stood out is **John Automobile** located in Mvog-Ada, Yaoundé. This shop sells parts for many car brands like Toyota, Honda, Mercedes, Hyundai, and Peugeot. They serve lots of customers every day, but they still use manual systems to track their inventory. When a customer calls to ask for a part, the staff have to walk to the warehouse and search physically. This takes too long and customers often go to another shop instead.

I chose John Automobile as my case study because it represents the many spare parts shops in Yaoundé that still work the old way. If I can help one shop, I can help many more.

## 1.2 Statement of the Problem

John Automobile has a big problem. They still use manual systems to manage their business. Every time a customer comes to ask for a part, the salesperson has to walk to the warehouse and look for it. If they find it, they quote a price from memory. If they do not find it, the customer goes away unhappy.

This manual system causes many problems. First, price is not the same for everyone. One customer might pay 10,000 XAF for a part while another pays 15,000 XAF for the same thing. This happens because there is no standard price list. Second, the shop owner does not know what parts they have in stock until someone goes to count them. Third, customers who call on the phone often wait a long time because staff have to search physically.

The biggest problem is that the shop loses customers every day because of this slow service. People in Yaoundé are busy and do not have time to wait. They want quick answers. Without a digital system, John Automobile cannot compete with other shops that might have better service.

So the main question I wanted to answer is: **How can I design and implement a web-based system that helps spare parts shops like John Automobile manage their inventory better and serve customers faster?**

## 1.3 Objectives of the Study

### 1.3.1 Main Objective

The main objective of this study is to design and implement a web-based car parts availability and price comparison system for spare parts vendors in Yaoundé, with John Automobile as the case study. The system will help vendors list their products online and help customers find and compare prices easily.

### 1.3.2 Specific Objectives

The specific objectives of this study are:

1. To examine the current manual processes used by spare parts shops in Yaoundé and identify the key problems that affect customer service and inventory management.

2. To design and develop a user-friendly web platform that allows vendors to add, update, and manage their product listings with prices and pictures.

3. To implement a search system that lets users find car parts by vehicle make, model, year, and category, with price comparison from different vendors.

4. To evaluate how the digital system improves the way spare parts shops operate and how customers respond to the new technology.

## 1.4 Research Questions

### 1.4.1 General Research Question

How can a web-based car parts availability and price comparison system improve the efficiency of spare parts vendors in Yaoundé, Cameroon?

### 1.4.2 Specific Research Questions

The specific research questions for this study are:

1. What are the main challenges that customers and vendors face when searching for and selling car parts in the Yaoundé market?

2. How can a web-based system be designed to provide real-time availability and side-by-side price comparison for car parts?

3. What impact does a digital inventory system have on the daily operations of spare parts shops like John Automobile?

4. How do users in Yaoundé respond to a digital platform for buying car parts, and what factors influence their acceptance?

## 1.5 Research Hypotheses

### 1.5.1 Specific Hypotheses

Based on the problem identified, the following hypotheses are proposed:

**H₀₁:** The manual inventory and pricing system used by spare parts shops in Yaoundé does not significantly affect their operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.

**H₁₁:** The manual inventory and pricing system used by spare parts shops in Yaoundé significantly affects their operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.

**H₀₂:** The implementation of a web-based car parts availability and price comparison system does not significantly improve the sales performance of spare parts vendors in Yaoundé.

**H₁₂:** The implementation of a web-based car parts availability and price comparison system significantly improves the sales performance of spare parts vendors in Yaoundé.

## 1.6 Significance of the Study

### 1.6.1 To the Automotive Industry

This study shows that digital transformation is possible for small spare parts shops in Cameroon. It provides a template for how traditional businesses can use web technology without spending too much money. The automotive industry in Cameroon can benefit from more shops going digital because it will create a connected market where information flows better.

### 1.6.2 To Vendors and Customers

For vendors like John Automobile, this system gives them a way to reach more customers through their phones. They no longer rely only on people who walk into their shop. For customers, they can save time by searching online first before they go to buy. They can also compare prices to make sure they get a fair deal.

According to IMARC Group (2025), the e-commerce market in Africa is worth billions and growing fast. By implementing systems like SparePlug, Cameroon can be part of this growth and help local businesses compete.

## 1.7 Justifications of the Study

I chose this topic because I saw the problem firsthand during my time in Yaoundé. Every day, people spend hours looking for car parts. They walk from shop to shop, ask the same question over and over, and often get different answers. This is very stressful and wastes a lot of time.

Also, Cameroon internet penetration is growing. More people have smartphones now. This means the time is right for a system like SparePlug. If I do not do this project now, someone else might do it later. But I want to be the one to help my country solve this problem.

The study is also important because it combines what I learned in school with real world problems. I used PHP, MySQL, and web design skills to build something that actually helps people. This makes my education useful.

## 1.8 Delimitation of the Study

### 1.8.1 Thematic Scope

This study focuses only on car parts availability and price comparison. It does not cover vehicle repair services, car insurance, or any other automotive services. The main function is product listing, searching, price comparison, and inquiry management.

### 1.8.2 Geographical Scope

The study takes place in Yaoundé, Cameroon. I focus on the Mvog-Ada area where John Automobile is located. While the system can work for all of Cameroon, the research data comes from Yaoundé only.

### 1.8.3 Time Scope

The practical work for this project happened during my internship period, which was from June 9th to August 29th, 2024. This is about 12 weeks. Within this time, I had to design, develop, and test the system. The timeline was short but enough to complete a working prototype.
