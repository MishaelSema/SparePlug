## PART 1: QUESTIONS FOR YOUR RELATIVE (THE VENDOR)

### Section A: Business Information

1. What is the name of your shop?
2. How long have you been selling car parts?
3. Where is your shop located? (Neighborhood/City)
4. What are your main car brands you sell parts for? (Toyota, Honda, Mercedes, etc.)
5. What types of parts do you sell most? (Engine, Brakes, Electrical, Body, etc.)
6. How many products do you have in your inventory roughly?
7. Do you have a physical shop or do you sell from home?

### Section B: Daily Operations

8. How many customers do you get per day on average?
9. What time of day do you get the most customers?
10. How do customers usually find out about your shop? (Word of mouth, Facebook, WhatsApp, passing by)
11. Do customers usually call ahead or just come to the shop?
12. What percentage of your customers are repeat customers?

### Section C: Current Problems

13. What is the biggest challenge you face in your business?
14. How do you currently track what parts you have in stock?
15. Do you ever forget what you have in stock and miss a sale?
16. How do you decide the prices for your products?
17. Do you charge different prices to different customers for the same product?
18. What happens when a customer asks for a part you don't have?
19. Do you lose customers because you don't have what they need?
20. How do customers pay you? (Cash, Mobile Money, Transfer)

### Section D: Customer Interaction

21. How do customers usually ask for parts? (Show up, call, WhatsApp)
22. What information do you need from customers to find the right part?
23. How long does it take you to find a part in your shop?
24. Do customers ever complain about prices being too high?
25. What do customers complain about most?

### Section E: Technology Use

26. Do you use any computer or phone app to manage your business?
27. Do you have a Facebook page or WhatsApp business account?
28. Do you post products online?
29. Have you ever tried to sell parts online before? How did it go?
30. What do you think about selling parts through a website or app?

### Section F: Dreams and Improvements

31. What would make your business better?
32. If you could have any tool to help your business, what would it be?
33. Would you be willing to list your products on a website where customers can find them?
34. How much time do you spend per day managing your inventory?
35. Would you pay a small monthly fee for a system that helps you manage your stock and get more customers?

## PART 2: QUESTIONS FOR CUSTOMERS

### Section A: Buying Behavior

1. How often do you need to buy car parts?
2. Where do you usually buy car parts from?
3. How do you find the shop you go to?
4. Have you ever bought car parts online before? (Facebook, WhatsApp, website)
5. What do you prefer: going to the shop or buying online? Why?

### Section B: Pain Points

6. How long does it take you to find the part you need?
7. Have you ever visited multiple shops just to find one part?
8. Have you ever bought a part that didn't fit your car? What happened?
9. Do you feel like prices are fair or do you have to negotiate every time?
10. Have you ever felt like a shop person was cheating you on price?

### Section C: Information Seeking

11. When you need a part, what information do you have ready? (Car model, part name, etc.)
12. Do you know what parts your car needs before you go to buy?
13. How do you know if a part will fit your car?

### Section D: Technology Preferences

14. Would you use a website or app to search for car parts?
15. What features would you want in such an app?
16. Would you compare prices from different shops if you could?
17. How would you want to contact the seller? (Call, WhatsApp, In-app message)
18. What would make you trust an online parts seller?

## PART 3: OBSERVATION GUIDE

### Things to Observe When You Visit the Shop

**Physical Setup:**
- How is the shop organized?
- Where are parts stored? (Shelves, boxes, floor)
- Do you have a catalog or list of what you sell?
- Is there a computer or smartphone being used?

**Customer Interaction:**
- Watch how customers ask for parts
- Notice how the vendor searches for parts
- Watch how prices are communicated
- Observe the negotiation process

**Record Keeping:**
- Look at any notebooks or papers used
- Check if there's a digital list anywhere
- Notice how sales are recorded

**Inventory Check:**
- Ask to see a sample of the inventory records
- Ask how they know what they have in stock
- Ask what happens when they run out of popular items

## INTERVIEW TIPS

### Before You Go:
1. Book an appointment first - don't just show up
2. Bring a notebook and pen
3. Bring your phone for recording (ask permission first)
4. Prepare your questions in advance
5. Bring a small gift (drink, food) as a sign of respect

### During the Interview:
1. Start with casual conversation to make them comfortable
2. Explain who you are and why you are asking
3. Don't rush - let them explain in detail
4. Ask follow-up questions
5. Take notes but maintain eye contact
6. Ask for permission to visit again

### After the Interview:
1. Write down everything you remember while it's fresh
2. Organize your notes by topic
3. Fill in any gaps you might have
4. Thank them again via WhatsApp or call
